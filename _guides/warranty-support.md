## Service and repairs

{% include warn.html text="The heater has no user-serviceable parts. All service and repairs beyond basic cleaning must be done by Focal-authorized personnel." %}

If a unit is damaged or malfunctioning, discontinue use, secure any loose parts, and contact Focal for a loaner or replacement rather than attempting a repair.

## Product reference

The Focal Duo is a cord-connected electric heater for covered indoor and outdoor use, running on 120VAC, 60 Hz. It conforms to UL 2021 and is certified by TÜV Rheinland.

## Disposal

The product contains recyclable materials. Don't dispose of it with household waste — contact Focal for returns and disposal.

## California Prop 65

WARNING: If not installed, operated, and maintained per Focal's instructions, this product can expose you to chemicals including nickel, known to the State of California to cause cancer. More information at www.P65Warnings.ca.gov.
