## How to request deletion

**In the app.** Open the Focal Home app, go to Settings, tap **Delete account** and follow the instructions in the app.

**Without the app.** Email [support@focalheat.co](mailto:support@focalheat.co?subject=Delete%20my%20Focal%20Home%20account) from the email address on your account. Use the subject line "Delete my Focal Home account". Focal Support handles these requests the same way as requests from the app.

## What gets deleted

Your Focal Home account and its data are removed. Device logs may be kept for diagnostics.

## What happens to your Duos

Your Duos are no longer tied to your account and may need to be factory reset before they can be set up on another account.

## How long it takes

Account deletion will be completed within 30 days.
