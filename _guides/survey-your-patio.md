## Why we ask

Focal uses your floor plan, photos, and height to draw a Preliminary Layout: where the rails and heaters go, and what your electrician and installer need to know.

## What you'll need

{% include checklist.html items="Pen and paper, <strong>or</strong> a floor plan you already have | Any phone, for the photos and the form | A tape measure, 25 ft or longer if you have one | About 15 minutes" %}

## The order to do it in

Do everything in this guide **before** you open the form. The form asks for each piece in turn, so it goes faster when they're ready.

1. [Draw or find a floor plan](#step-1-draw-or-find-a-floor-plan).
2. [Take the photos](#step-2-take-the-photos).
3. [Measure the height](#step-3-measure-the-height).
4. [Fill out the Focal Site Survey form](#step-4-fill-out-the-form).

## More than one seating area?

If you have more than one seating area, like a patio and a parklet, do Steps 1 to 3 for each one, then fill out the form once per area. The form asks each area's name and how many areas you have.

## Step 1: Draw or find a floor plan

A plan you already have is best: a seating chart, a permit drawing, or an architect's drawing. If you don't have one, draw the seating area on paper and take a photo of it to upload in the form.

Either way, check that it has:

{% include checklist.html items="A length on every side. On the entrance side, that's the pieces on each side of the opening | Each table as a box, with its size in inches | A small circle for each chair, on the side where it sits | The entrance: a gap in the outline and an arrow | Anything built in, like benches or planters, marked <strong>built in</strong>" %}

Tables not set up yet? Draw them where they'll go. Free-standing tables can shift a little to fit the heaters, but built-ins can't move, which is why we ask you to mark them.

{% include diagram.html src="survey-sketch-example.svg" caption="Example sketch: a length on every side, including the pieces on each side of the entrance, tables with their sizes, and a circle for each chair." %}

## Step 2: Take the photos

You'll take three photos on the regular **1x** lens. The **0.5x** wide lens bends straight lines, so step back to fit the seating area in instead. If there's no room to step back, 0.5x is okay.


| Photo           | How to take it                                                                                                             |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Front view**  | The whole seating area, straight on, from the side with the entrance. It goes on your layout's cover                      |
| **Corner view** | From a back corner, across from the entrance, showing the back and one side of the seating area. **Don't draw on it**     |
| **Looking up**  | From the middle of the seating area, pointing up at what's overhead. Take it even if nothing is overhead                  |

## Step 3: Measure the height

Measure **H**: from the floor up to the lowest beam or awning frame above the tables. Feet and inches are fine, e.g. *8 ft 6 in*. No decimals needed.

Nothing overhead? Write *none*.

## Step 4: Fill out the form

When your floor plan, photos, and height are ready, [open the Focal Site Survey form](https://form.typeform.com/to/DQXI87AN). Answer the questions, upload the floor plan and photos, and enter the height.

More than one seating area? Fill out the form once for each area.

## Still stuck?

If you can't get a floor plan or photos to work, or you'd like help planning where the tables go, a Focal team member will set up a guided video call and walk through your patio with you. Contact your sales team member or email [hello@focalheat.co](mailto:hello@focalheat.co).

## What happens next

We'll email your Preliminary Layout within 2 business days of your form. If anything is missing from the floor plan or photos, we'll let you know.

Questions? Contact your sales team member or email [hello@focalheat.co](mailto:hello@focalheat.co).
