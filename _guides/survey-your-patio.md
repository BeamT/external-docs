## Why we ask

Focal uses your scan (or sketch), photos, and measurements to draw a Preliminary Layout: where the rails and heaters go, and what your electrician and installer need to know.

## What you'll need

{% include checklist.html items="An iPhone with Polycam for the scan, <strong>or</strong> pen and paper for a sketch | For a scan: tables and chairs set up for service | Any phone, for the photos and the form | A tape measure, 25 ft or longer if you have one | About 15 minutes for the form" %}

## The order to do it in

Do everything in this guide **before** you open the form. The form asks for each piece in turn, so it goes faster when they're ready.

1. [Scan or sketch your patio](#step-1-scan-or-sketch-your-patio).

3. [Measure and mark up the corner photo](#step-3-measure-and-mark-up-the-corner-photo).
4. [Fill out the Focal Site Survey form](#step-4-fill-out-the-form).

## Step 1: Scan or sketch your patio

You need one: a Polycam scan **or** a sketch. **We recommend the scan.** It's much faster, and it shows us more.

### Are your tables set up for service?

The scan needs the tables and chairs out where they go for service: we place the heaters from them.

- **Set up?** [Scan your patio](#scan-your-patio).
- **Not set up?** Set them up and scan, or [draw a sketch](#draw-a-sketch) showing where they'll go.
- **No iPhone?** Polycam's Space mode isn't on Android, so [draw a sketch](#draw-a-sketch). Or ask anyone on your staff with an iPhone to scan and send you the link.

### Scan your patio

[Get Polycam free from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/polycam-3d-scanner-lidar-360/id1532482376), then open it and create a free account.

Scan the whole patio in one go. Polycam may not warn you about a missed spot, so use this list as your check:

{% include checklist.html items="The whole patio, end to end | Anything overhead: an awning, canopy, roof, or beams. Point your phone up as well as around | Walls (including the building, down to the floor), posts, railings, and planters | Tables and chairs, and their edges" %}

{% include step.html number="1" title="Start in a corner" body="Open Polycam, start a new capture, and choose <strong>Space</strong> (not Floorplan). Stand in a corner of the patio, next to the building if there is one, hold your phone upright at chest height, and start." %}

{% include step.html number="2" title="Walk the edge slowly, facing in" body="Walk the edge of the patio at a slow, steady pace, <strong>facing into the area</strong>. As you go, tilt the phone up to catch the building wall and anything overhead, then down to the floor edges. Don't stand in one spot and turn: the app needs views from different spots." %}

{% include step.html number="3" title="Close the loop" body="Keep going to the far end and back to your starting corner, then finish the capture." %}

### Wait for processing

After you finish, Polycam processes the scan. If it asks where to process, choose **Cloud**, so you can share the link. **This can take a while.** Keep the app open and your phone on WiFi if you can.

- It's done when the scan opens as a 3D model you can spin around.
- While you wait, move on to [the photos](#step-2-take-the-photos) and [measurements](#step-3-measure-and-mark-up-the-corner-photo).

### Copy the Polycam link

Once processing has finished:

{% include step.html number="1" title="Open your scan" body="In Polycam, open the scan of your patio." %}

{% include step.html number="2" title="Copy the link" body="Tap <strong>Share</strong>, then copy the link. You'll paste it into the form's Polycam question. If someone else is filling out the form, text or email them the link." %}

{% include warn.html text="Wait until the scan has finished processing before you copy the link." %}

### Draw a sketch

Scanned your patio with the tables set up? Skip the sketch and go to [Step 2](#step-2-take-the-photos).

Draw your patio on paper and take a photo of it to upload in the form. Measure each side with the tape and write the lengths on the sketch. Tables not set up yet? Draw them where they'll go.

{% include checklist.html items="A length on every line you draw. On the entrance side, that's the pieces on each side of the opening | Each table as a box, with its size in inches | A small circle for each chair, on the side where it sits | The entrance: leave a gap in the outline and draw an arrow" %}

## Step 2: Take the photos

You'll take three photos. Use your phone's normal camera, not Polycam, on the regular **1x** lens. The **0.5x** wide lens bends straight lines, so step back to fit the patio in instead. If there's no room to step back, 0.5x is okay.


| Photo            | How to take it                                                                                                              |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Front view**   | The whole patio, straight on, with the building and anything overhead. **Don't draw on it**: it goes on your layout's cover |
| **Side view**    | Stand at one end and shoot along the long side, so we can see the height and what's overhead                                |
| **Corner photo** | One more photo from a corner, showing the front and side of the patio. You'll mark this one up next                         |

## Step 3: Measure and mark up the corner photo

Do this even if you scanned or sketched. Tape measurements let us check the scan or sketch against real numbers, and the photo shows the height overhead, which a sketch can't.

{% include diagram.html src="survey-corner-markup.svg" fill=true caption="Corner photo: W and D along the floor of the seating area, H from the floor up to what's overhead." %}

### Measure

Take three measurements of the seating area. Each number in the form matches one of them.

- **W:** along the floor, from one side of the seating area to the other.
- **D:** along the floor, from the building (or back edge) to the front edge.
- **H:** from the floor up to the lowest beam, awning, or roof. Nothing overhead? Skip H.

Start and end each one at something you can see, like a wall, railing, planter, or curb. Feet and inches are fine, e.g. *18 ft 6 in*. No decimals needed.

### Mark up the corner photo

On the corner photo, draw a line for each measurement, from the same start point to the same end point, and label it with its letter and number, e.g. *W 18 ft 6 in*.

To draw on the photo:

- **iPhone:** Photos → Edit → Markup
- **Android:** Google Photos → Edit → Markup

{% include warn.html text="Draw only on the corner photo. Keep the front and side photos clean." %}

## Step 4: Fill out the form

When your photos, measurements, and Polycam link (or sketch) are ready, [open the Focal Site Survey form](https://form.typeform.com/to/DQXI87AN). Answer the questions, upload the photos, and paste the Polycam link or upload a photo of your sketch.

## Still stuck?

If you can't get a scan or a sketch to work, or you'd like help planning where the tables go, a Focal team member will set up a guided video call and walk through your patio with you. Contact your sales team member or email [hello@focalheat.co](mailto:hello@focalheat.co).

## What happens next

We'll email your Preliminary Layout within 2 business days of your form. If anything is missing from the scan, sketch, or photos, we'll let you know.
