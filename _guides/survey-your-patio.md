## Why we ask

Focal uses your scan, photos, and measurements to draw a Preliminary Layout: where the rails and heaters go, and what your electrician and installer need to know.

## The order to do it in

Do everything in this guide **before** you open the form. The form asks for each piece in turn, so it goes faster when they're ready.

1. Scan your patio in Polycam on an iPhone and let it process.
2. Take the photos.
3. Mark up the corner photo.
4. Measure.
5. Draw a sketch, if your patio isn't a rectangle.
6. Open the [Focal Site Survey form](https://form.typeform.com/to/DQXI87AN), answer the questions, upload the photos and paste the Polycam link. Tables and chairs not set up? Don't fill out the form yet: see [Tables not set up yet?](#tables-not-set-up-yet)

## Scan your patio

The scan needs an **iPhone** (Polycam's Space mode isn't on Android). Anyone on your staff can do it and send you the link. [Get Polycam free from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/polycam-3d-scanner-lidar-360/id1532482376), then open it and create a free account.

Scan the whole patio in one go. Polycam may not warn you about a missed spot, so use this list as your check:

{% include checklist.html items="The whole patio, end to end | Anything overhead: an awning, canopy, roof, or beams. Point your phone up as well as around | Walls, posts, railings, and planters | Tables and chairs, and their edges | Where the building meets the patio" %}

## Wait for processing

After you finish, Polycam processes the scan. If it asks where to process, choose **Cloud**, so you can share the link. **This can take a while.** Keep the app open and your phone on WiFi if you can.

- It's done when the scan opens as a 3D model you can spin around.
- Don't copy the share link until then.
- While you wait, take the photos and measurements below.

## Take the photos

You'll take three photos. Use your phone's normal camera, not Polycam, on the regular **1x** lens. The **0.5x** wide lens bends straight lines, so step back to fit the patio in instead. If there's no room to step back, 0.5x is okay.


| Photo            | How to take it                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Front view**   | Stand back far enough to fit the whole patio in one shot, facing it straight on (usually from the street or the entrance). Include the building behind it and anything overhead. **Don't draw on this one**: it goes on the cover of your layout |
| **Side view**    | Stand at one end and shoot along the long side, so we can see the height and what's overhead                                                                                                                                     |
| **Corner photo** | One more photo from a corner, showing the front and side of the patio. You'll draw on this one next                                                                                                                       |

## Mark what you'll measure

{% include diagram.html src="survey-corner-markup.svg" fill=true caption="Corner photo: W and D along the floor of the seating area, H from the floor up to what's overhead." %}

On the corner photo, draw and label three lines. Each number in the form matches one of them.

- **W:** along the floor, from one side of the seating area to the other.
- **D:** along the floor, from the building (or back edge) to the front edge.
- **H:** from the floor up to the lowest beam, awning, or roof. Nothing overhead? Skip H.

End each line at something you can see, like a wall, railing, planter, or curb.

To draw on the photo:

- **iPhone:** Photos → Edit → Markup
- **Android:** Google Photos → Edit → Markup

{% include warn.html text="Draw only on the corner photo. Keep the front and side photos clean." %}

## Measure

Measure each line you drew on the corner photo: W, D, and H if you drew it.

- Measure **exactly the lines you drew**, from the same start point to the same end point, so each number matches the photo.
- Feet and inches are fine, e.g. *18 ft 6 in*. No decimals needed.
- Write the three numbers down, or keep them on the corner photo.

## Sketch, if you need one

You only need a sketch if **your patio isn't a rectangle**. L-shapes, angled sides, or more than 4 sides count. Draw the shape, leave a gap for the entrance, and **write the length on every side**. Measure each side while the tape is out. A photo of a hand drawing on paper is fine.

## Tables not set up yet?

If your patio is new or the furniture is put away, do the scan, photos, and measurements anyway. **Don't fill out the form yet.** Instead, **contact your sales team member or email [hello@focalheat.co](mailto:hello@focalheat.co)**, and a Focal team member will set up a video call to plan where the tables go.

## Can't get a scan?

If no one on your staff has an iPhone, the scan won't work, or we find it doesn't show enough, a Focal team member will set up a guided video call and walk through your patio with you.

To ask for one, **contact your sales team member or email [hello@focalheat.co](mailto:hello@focalheat.co).**

## Fill out the form

When your photos, measurements, and Polycam link are ready, [**open the Focal Site Survey form**](https://form.typeform.com/to/DQXI87AN).

## What happens next

We'll email your Preliminary Layout within 2 business days of your form. If anything is missing from the scan or photos, we'll let you know.

Questions? Contact your sales team member or email [hello@focalheat.co](mailto:hello@focalheat.co).
