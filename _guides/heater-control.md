## Logging in

You'll get a unique login link and password from Focal by email. Open the link, enter your password, and you're in — there's no username, since the link is specific to your space.

## Accessing the menu

Tap the three lines in the upper corner, next to the Focal logo, to open the menu. You'll see three options: **Heater Control**, **Scheduling**, and **Settings**. Scheduling and Settings are where you set things up; Heater Control is where you run day to day.

## The two modes

Focal Control runs your heaters one of two ways. You pick the mode from the Scheduling menu.

{% include dodont.html do="Use Schedule Mode day to day — it's the most energy-efficient and runs automatically." dont="Leave heaters in Manual Mode by default — they won't adjust or shut off on their own." %}

**Schedule Mode** turns heaters on and off at the start and end of your hours, and adjusts heat automatically based on whether a guest is seated. **Manual Mode** hands full control to staff — heaters stay exactly where you set them until someone changes them. Manual Mode is useful when you need to run outside your normal hours: switch to it, run the heaters, then switch back without changing your saved schedule.

## Set your schedule

From the menu, tap **Scheduling**, then check **Enable Schedule** to turn on Schedule Mode.

## Set your heat levels

From the menu, tap **Settings**. You'll see two heat levels that apply in Schedule Mode: heat runs from OFF (0) to HIGH (3).

**Unoccupied Heat Level** is what heaters hold when no one's seated. **Occupied Heat Level** is what they jump to when a guest sits down — the guest can then adjust their own side from there. Change these anytime as conditions change.

## The Heater Control page

Open it from the menu. This is where you'll spend most of your time. It has a few areas:

The **Dashboard** shows the mode your heaters are in. In Schedule Mode it shows whether the schedule is active and what time heaters shut down, plus a button to switch to Manual Mode. **System Status** shows how many heaters are online and active. The **power button** turns all heaters on or off at once.

The **Restaurant Map** shows where your heaters sit. If you have multiple parklets or areas, Focal can set these up as separate **zones** you control as a group.

**All Heaters and Zone controls** push a heat level to every heater, or to every heater in one zone at once — handy for warming the whole space quickly.

**Zone maps** below that show each zone with the true position of every heater on the rail, so units are easy to identify. Tap a heater to open its individual controls.

## Common tasks

**Skip an upcoming holiday.** Menu → Scheduling → uncheck the closed days → Save Settings. Heaters won't activate on those days.

**Pre-heat before a shift.** Menu → Settings → set Unoccupied Heat Level to High → Save Settings. The space warms up before guests arrive.

**Run outside your hours once.** From the dashboard, switch to Manual Mode, run the heaters, then switch back to Schedule Mode — your saved hours are untouched.

## Guest QR control

Guests can scan the QR code on the heater pendant to open a simple page on their phone and adjust their own heat level, without needing staff.

## How Schedule Mode behaves

Outside your scheduled hours, heaters can't be controlled by the app or pull-string until you adjust the schedule or switch to Manual Mode. During those times a heater shows as disabled, its purple and orange lights are off, and it flashes green if someone pulls the string. When a guest sits down the heater adjusts immediately to the occupied level; when they leave, it returns to the unoccupied level after about five minutes.
