## Before you start

Read [Safety & Clearances]({{ site.baseurl }}/safety-and-clearances/) before you begin. It covers the minimum clearances this install has to meet.

The site plan specifies where each rail goes and how long it is. Confirm the exact location against the plan before you mount anything.

Heaters slide on and off only at the opening end of the rail, the end opposite the cord-exit end. Leave at least 4" past the opening end to slide a heater on, clear of walls, posts and beams.

{% include dodont.html do="Confirm the mounting surface is solid and can hold 300 lb" dont="Mount to anything that flexes or isn't rated for the load." %}

{% include warn.html text="Do not power on the rail until mounting is complete and clearances are verified." %}

## About the rails

The rail holds the individual Focal heaters, which can be installed after the rail is up. Rails come in three lengths:

{% include railtable.html %}

Each rail has a T-slot running its length for T-nuts, and an 8-foot built-in power cable. Focal offers mounting hardware but off-the-shelf hardware also works utilizing the T-slot.

## What's in the mounting kit

Every rail ships with the same kit: two pre-built rail blocks, two of each overhead bracket, plus fastener options for whichever mount and fastening method suit your structure.

### Rail blocks

Two per rail, pre-built. These slide into the rail's T-slot, and the dropdown pipes seat into them.

| Part | Qty |
|---|---|
| Parallel bracket | 1 |
| T-nut, 1/4-20 | 1 |
| Set screw, 1/4-20 x 5/16" | 2 |
| Flat-head screw, 1/4-20 x 1.125" | 2 |

### Overhead brackets and pipe

The brackets fasten to the structure and hold the top of each dropdown pipe. Pick one type, parallel or perpendicular, and use two per rail. See [Mount the rail](#mount-the-rail).

| Part | Qty | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Parallel bracket | 2 | Ceiling mount, underside of a member |
| Perpendicular bracket | 2 | Beam/joist mount, vertical face |
| Set screw, 1/4-20 x 5/16" | 4 | Clamps pipe into bracket |
| Dropdown pipe, 6 ft | 1 | Cut to drop length (up to 3 ft) |

### Through-mount fasteners

Nut on the far side. Pick the screw length for your material thickness.

| Part | Qty |
|---|---|
| Flat-head screw, 1/4-20 x 1.5"/2.5"/3.5" | 4 each |
| SAE washer, 1/4" ID | 4 |
| Nylon lock nut, 1/4-20 | 4 |

### Wood screws

For solid wood where you can't reach the far side.

| Part | Qty |
|---|---|
| Wood screw, #12 x 2"/3" | 4 each |

### Tools

One of each.

| Tool | Drives |
|---|---|
| 1/8" L-wrench | Set screws |
| T30 L-wrench | Machine screws (1/4-20 flat-head) |
| T25 screwdriver bit | Wood screws |


## Rail placement over the table

Ideally the rail runs down the center of the table, so heat hits the middle of each guest's chest, not the top of their head. If the rail has to be off-center, Focal can angle the heaters toward the guests. Contact Focal to arrange it.

{% include step.html number="2B" title="Prepare a beam or joist mount" body="Use this where the only solid surface is a vertical face, such as the side of a beam or joist. Repeat for the second mount, matching the drop length so the rail hangs level." diagram="rail-02b-drill-hole.png,rail-02b-screw-bracket.png,rail-02b-insert-pipe.png,rail-02b-set-screws.png" grid="true" %}

{% include step.html number="3" title="Attach the rail to the pipes" body="With a second person, lift the rail up under the pipes (1). ## Different mounting methods

Any mounting hardware must connect to the rail through T-nuts, but some sites need a different approach:

- A threaded rod as a drop-down
- A U-bolt to attach unistrut to a pipe
- Mounting directly through a wood beam without a drop-down, using the longer 1/4-20 flat-head screws with SAE washers and nylon lock nuts
- An L-bracket to unistrut when rails run perpendicular to a wall

## Plug in the rail

{% include dodont.html do="Plug directly into a dedicated, GFCI-protected outdoor outlet on its own circuit." dont="Use an extension cord, power strip, or a circuit shared with other loads." %}

Run the cable along the rail, up the mounting hardware, or along parklet features so it stays out of sight from diners and the street. Plug into the outlet, then turn on the rail switch. It's on the cord-exit end, underneath the rail. Secure any slack so nothing hangs below the bottom of the rail.
