## Read this first

Failure to follow these instructions could result in fire, electric shock, injury, or death. Keep this document with the heater for future reference.

{% include warn.html text="The heater surface can exceed 70°C during operation. The only surface anyone should touch is the pull-cord." %}

## Minimum clearances

Every install must meet these distances. Measurements are taken from the heater and rail to the nearest surface.

| Reference | Minimum distance |
|---|---|
| Ceiling, above rail | 0 in (0 cm) |
| Wall, side of rail | 20 in (51 cm) |
| Objects under the heater | 36 in (91 cm) |
| Floor to bottom of rail | 84 in / 7 ft minimum — use 90 in where people walk underneath |
| Wall to side of heater facing down | 20 in (51 cm) |

## Do and don't

{% include dodont.html do="Mount above the table surface, out of reach of normal use." dont="Mount where people brush against the heater or its hot parts." %}

{% include dodont.html do="Keep awnings, umbrellas, and fabric at least 20 in from the heater." dont="Hang or drape anything on or near the rail or heaters." %}

{% include dodont.html do="Plug directly into a dedicated, GFCI-protected outdoor outlet." dont="Use an extension cord or power strip, or share the circuit." %}

## Electrical

The Focal Duo runs on 120VAC, 60 Hz. Each rail ships with a factory-installed 15 A or 20 A plug depending on how many Duos it carries. Match the outlet and breaker to the plug, and give each rail its own dedicated circuit.

{% include warn.html text="Do not power on the heater until installation is complete and clearances are verified." %}

## When to stop and call Focal

Discontinue use and contact Focal Support if you see physical damage to the housing or grill, a burned or frayed cord, unusual smells or sounds, or a heater that won't respond to controls.
