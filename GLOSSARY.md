# Focal Support Site

The public support site (support.focalheat.co) that tells customers, installers and electricians how to install, set up, control and troubleshoot Focal heaters. It serves more than one product, so its language separates what is sold from where the reader is in the journey.

## Language

**Product**:
A line Focal sells, which decides which guides apply. There are two: Restaurant and Home.
_Avoid_: Variant, edition, consumer, commercial

**Restaurant**:
The Product sold to restaurants and other commercial venues. It uses a Focal Hub and the waitstaff UI.
_Avoid_: Commercial, Pro

**Home**:
The Product sold to homeowners (Focal Home). Each Duo joins the home Wi-Fi directly and is set up and controlled in a mobile app, with no Focal Hub and no waitstaff UI.
_Avoid_: Consumer, Duo for Homes

**Stage**:
Where a Guide sits in the customer's journey, shown as a tab on the landing page: Install, Operate or Service. A Guide can sit in more than one Stage, or in none and appear only under All. Stages are the same for every Product.
_Avoid_: Role, audience, phase (reserved for launch phases)

**Install**:
The Stage for getting heaters mounted, powered and commissioned: electrical, rails, heaters and, for Restaurant, the Hub and assigning.
_Avoid_: Setup (reserved for the Home app flow)

**Operate**:
The Stage for setting up in the app and controlling heaters day to day.
_Avoid_: Use, control

**Service**:
The Stage for keeping it running after install: swaps, troubleshooting, maintenance, warranty and changing or moving a Duo.
_Avoid_: Support, repair

**Installer**:
A person mounting the rail and heaters, either a hired professional or the customer doing it themselves. They are done when the rail and heaters have power. Not a navigation label: Stages replace per-reader pills.
_Avoid_: Contractor, technician

**Mount**:
The way a rail attaches to the structure. Home launches with Overhead only. Freestanding, Wall and Self-mount may follow.
_Avoid_: Mounting method, mount type

**Overhead mount**:
The Mount where the rail hangs from a ceiling or beam on dropdown pipes. At launch it is a section of the Install Rails guide.
_Avoid_: Ceiling mount, dropdown mount

**Guide**:
One page of instructions in the support site, tagged for one or more Products and Stages.
_Avoid_: Article, doc, manual

**Fix it**:
The step-by-step troubleshooting tool at /fixit/. It serves the Restaurant Product only. Home help is intended to live in the mobile app.
_Avoid_: Troubleshooter, wizard
